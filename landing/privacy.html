Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

Plain English Privacy Commitment

MicMorph is built from the ground up on an absolute privacy-first architecture. 100% of your audio processing happens entirely locally on your device in real time. We do not record, store, transcribe, or transmit your voice or microphone data to any cloud server or third party. Your voice never leaves your computer.

1. Audio and Voice Privacy (Zero Voice Data Collection)

Your spoken words, voice timbre, and microphone stream are treated with the highest degree of privacy:

  • No Cloud Audio Transmission: Your microphone input and processed audio are never transmitted over the internet or sent to external servers, cloud services, or artificial intelligence inference APIs.
  • No Audio Recording or Disk Storage: MicMorph processes audio samples entirely in temporary volatile memory (RAM) and directly outputs them to the virtual audio device. The application does not write audio files, recordings, or voice transcripts to disk.
  • No Voice Fingerprinting: MicMorph does not generate, analyze, or retain biometric voice prints, acoustic profiles, or speaker identification templates.

2. Anonymous Telemetry & Usage Analytics (PostHog)

To ensure application stability, diagnose errors, measure performance, and understand how features are utilized, we collect usage data through PostHog (PostHog, Inc.) on our website and within our desktop application. PostHog processes this data on servers in the United States. We do not ask for your name or email address, and we do not link this data to an account.

What the desktop app collects:

  • Feature Interaction Events: When the app is opened, the pitch presets and values you choose (e.g. Subtle, Medium, Deep, Deepest), voice style (masculine or feminine), whether live preview is on, and theme changes.
  • Call Session Duration: The duration of each active morphing session (in seconds) to understand session length and prevent stability issues during extended calls.
  • Selected Microphone Name: The name of the input device you select (for example, “MacBook Pro Microphone”). If you have renamed a device, this name may include the name you gave it.
  • System Environment: Your operating system (macOS or Windows) and the application version.
  • Anonymous ID: A random identifier created when the app first runs and stored on your device, so that events from the same installation can be grouped together.

What our website collects:

  • Page and Interaction Events: Pages viewed, clicks (including download and install-command buttons), and use of the voice demo, such as samples played and presets chosen.
  • Session Recordings: Recordings of how you navigate the website, such as mouse movement, clicks, and scrolling, used to improve the website. They do not include audio.
  • Technical Information: Browser, device type, operating system, screen size, referring page, approximate location derived from your IP address, and errors that occur on the page.

What is NEVER collected:

  • We NEVER record, intercept, or transmit any audio, microphone data, spoken words, or transcripts.
  • We NEVER collect personal identifiers such as your full name, email address, physical address, phone number, or biometric data.
  • We NEVER sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your telemetry data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Computer

MicMorph stores a small amount of information strictly on your local device:

  • Usage File: A human-readable file (usage_config.json) in ~/Library/Application Support/com.micmorph.desktop/ on macOS, or %APPDATA%\com.micmorph.desktop\ on Windows. It contains only the date you last used MicMorph, how long you have used it that day, and your plan status.
  • App Preferences: Your light or dark theme preference and the anonymous ID described in section 2, kept in the application’s own local storage.

This information is stored so the application works as expected. Apart from the anonymous ID, which is sent with usage events, none of it is uploaded to any remote server.

4. Virtual Audio Drivers (BlackHole and VB-CABLE)

MicMorph routes processed audio locally through a virtual audio driver: BlackHole, an open-source Core Audio driver created by Existential Audio Inc., on macOS, and VB-CABLE, created by VB-Audio Software, on Windows. These drivers run locally on your computer and only pass audio between apps. MicMorph does not use them to collect or transmit any data.

5. Website Cookies and Local Storage

Our website uses local browser storage (such as localStorage) to remember your UI preference (dark mode vs. light mode). Our analytics provider, PostHog, also sets a first-party cookie and local storage entries to recognize returning visitors and group page views into sessions, as described in section 2. We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting pixels, or advertising networks. You can clear or block these cookies in your browser settings, and the website will continue to work.

6. Children's Privacy

MicMorph does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

7. GDPR & CCPA Compliance (Data Subject Rights)

Because MicMorph operates on an anonymous, local-first model, we do not associate telemetry data with your personal identity or account. However, to the extent applicable under data protection laws (such as GDPR or CCPA), you have the right to request information about our data practices or request cessation of telemetry. You may also block outbound network traffic from the desktop app using a standard firewall (such as Little Snitch or LuLu on macOS, or Windows Defender Firewall on Windows) without impacting real-time audio modulation functionality.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in our application or changes in legal requirements. We encourage you to review this page occasionally. Updates are effective upon publication.
