Questions, answered.

The short version: install it, pick a voice, and choose “MicMorph” as your microphone.

Which apps does MicMorph work with?

Any app that lets you choose a microphone, including Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Slack, Discord and OBS Studio. Select “MicMorph” as the microphone in the app’s audio settings.

Can people tell I’m using it?

At Subtle and Medium, most listeners hear it as your natural voice: MicMorph keeps your timing and the way you speak. Deep and Deepest are more noticeable, so try them with live preview first.

Is my voice recorded or uploaded?

No. Your audio is processed on your computer in real time and never leaves it. The app sends anonymous usage events, such as which preset and microphone you choose, to help us improve it. It never sends audio.

Which computers are supported?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or later), and 64-bit Windows 10 and 11.

Why does it need a virtual audio driver?

Call apps can only listen to microphones. The driver (BlackHole on Mac, VB-CABLE on Windows) adds a “MicMorph” microphone that carries your adjusted voice into the call. The install command sets it up for you.

Is the install command safe to run?

The script downloads MicMorph and the audio driver it needs, installs them, sets up the “MicMorph” microphone and opens the app. You can read the Mac script or the Windows script before you run it.

Will it slow my computer down?

MicMorph is a small native app built in Rust. It runs quietly in the background from your menu bar or system tray while you’re on a call.

Can I use it on my phone?

Not yet. MicMorph runs on Mac and Windows computers. On your phone, tap Share to send the install command to your computer.
